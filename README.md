One image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026, at
broadsheet density on a 1024x768 (4:3) page: a "Day in Numbers" band,
fifteen numbered story cards — each with a status/source tag and its
own small drawn scene — a dot world map keyed to the card numbers
("Where It Happened"), and a one-line briefs section ("Also on
20 November").

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the image beside
it to `/output/entry.png`, after checking the payload's PNG
signature, its size against the 1 MiB gate, and its 1024x768
dimensions. The image itself was drawn procedurally in the build
session with PIL: fixed text, fixed geometry, no external assets,
no network — two runs write byte-identical files.

Every card below is an event that happened, or was first reported,
on 20 November 2026 in some time zone. Follow-ups to Thursday's
stories are framed as Friday developments only where Friday brought
news of its own; Thursday's events themselves are listed under
"Considered but not printed" so the date discipline is auditable.

## The fifteen cards

1. **Russia pounds Kyiv with ballistic missiles.** Explosions and
   fires in the Obolonskyi, Podilskyi and Holosiivskyi districts
   before dawn on Friday; Mayor Klitschko reported damage to homes

2. **COP31 talks are extended as no deal is reached on Friday.**
   President Murat Kurum adjourns the evening plenary in Antalya
   with finance and fossil fuels unresolved; negotiating teams work
   overnight on a third draft due Saturday. Some decisions under
   Article 6 of the Paris Agreement are adopted, and 75 countries
   now back the draft's goal of raising electricity's share of
   final energy to 35% by 2035. *NO DEAL YET — Hürriyet / AFP*
3. **Asia slides as the AI rout ends in the worst week since
   2020.** The Nikkei 225 closes 5.1% down at 56,436.65 — its
   fifth-biggest single-day point drop and its lowest close since
   April — while the Kospi falls 5.2% to 4,869.96, Taiwan's Taiex
   plunges 6.6%, and MSCI's Asia-Pacific index sinks 8% on the
   week. Wall Street follows on Friday: the S&P 500 falls 3.7%
   and the Nasdaq 5.3%. *DATA — Reuters*
4. **Oil edges higher after Houthi missiles target Riyadh.** Brent
   settles at $92.57 a barrel on Friday, up 0.6% on the day, even
   as both benchmarks head for a third straight weekly decline;
   crude shipments through Hormuz have returned to roughly pre-war

5. **Trump defends the Xi–Putin summit at the White House.**

## Considered but not printed

Compiled during the build and held back, mostly on date grounds —
each was Thursday 19 November's news, already summed up by that
day's page, and printing it again as Friday's news would misdate it:

- The Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen, its four-point
  declaration, and the APEC leaders' Shenzhen Outlook (all Thu 19
  Nov) — only Friday's fallout, Trump's White House briefing
  defending the summit, is card 5.
- The ICJ's verdict that Myanmar committed genocide against the
  Rohingya (Thu 19 Nov) — only Friday's UN reaction is brief 17.
- Anthropic's trading debut: priced at $83, opened at $111.50,
  closed at $97.17 (Thu 19 Nov) — it appears on Friday's page only
  as context inside the market rout, card 3.
- Voyager 1's one-light-day milestone: crossed on Wed 18 Nov and
  confirmed by NASA on Thu 19 Nov when its signal arrived — not
  Friday news at all, however good the story.
- The US Senate's 48–51 rejection of the Iran war-powers
  resolution (Thu 19 Nov, US time).
- Florida's record 20th execution of the year and the Texas lethal
  injection (Thu 19 Nov, US time).
- Putin ruling out a Black Sea / energy truce (said Thu 19 Nov in
  Shenzhen) — Friday's Kyiv strikes, its sequel, lead the page
  instead as card 1.
- Pope Leo XIV closing his South America tour in Peru: the farewell
  Mass was on Monday 16 November, not Friday — a page index dated
  20 November is not the event's date.
- Paraguay's Senate rejecting President Peña's bid to delay his
  impeachment trial: that vote was on Friday 6 November, not
  20 November — same listing-date trap as the Pope item.
- El Salvador's President Bukele registering his third-term
  candidacy: registered on Thursday 19 November; Friday's reports
  are follow-ups, not a Friday event.
- The APEC ministers' joint statement as the draft's card 5: its
  substance was Thursday's leaders' Shenzhen Outlook. Friday's
  real APEC news was Trump's White House briefing defending the
  summit, which now leads as the final card 5.
- The White House tapping Kirsten Davies as cyber chief (Le Monde,
  20 Nov): a genuine Friday item, cut from the briefs in the final
  edition to make room for the Latin America brief — Latin oil
  shares falling on Friday (Petrobras −1.7%, YPF −2.9%, Rio Times).

Final edition: compiled on the evening of 21 November 2026 (CST),
after 20 November had ended in every time zone. The three
morning-draft risk points all moved and were re-based on closing
figures: COP31 ended Friday with no deal and talks extended into
Saturday; the Nikkei closed −5.06% and the Kospi −5.21%; the APEC
card was replaced by Friday's White House briefing. No card on
the final page is a Thursday event.
